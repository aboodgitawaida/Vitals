Before you play

    
STILL watches you while you play. With your permission it uses your webcam to measure your heart rate and read your face, and your microphone to hear you. The game changes depending on how scared you are. The monster can hear your heartbeat.

    
    
      
  • It contains intense jump scares, flashing light, sudden loud sounds and darkness.
    • 
      
  • Do not play if you have a heart condition, epilepsy or photosensitivity, or are pregnant.
    • 
      
  • If your measured heart rate stays very high, the game pauses for a break (you can change this in Settings).
    • 
      
  • Everything is processed on this device. No video, sound or heart data leaves your computer.
    • 
      
  • This is a game. The heart rate is not medical and not for health use.

How it works

    
Your webcam measures your heart rate from tiny colour changes in your skin (Vitals: a neural network trained on 400 people, running in your browser). Face tracking reads fast reactions: eyes widening, a flinch, looking away. The microphone hears gasps and screams.

    
A Director turns that into a fear score and treats every scare as an experiment: what made your heart rate climb, what made you flinch, and what you've stopped reacting to. It builds dread, waits for you to relax, then strikes with what works on you. When you're past your limit, it backs off.

    
And the thing in the building hears your heart. A calm heart is silent. A racing one carries through walls. Hiding works only if you can calm down.

    
At the end you get your fear profile: what scared you most.
